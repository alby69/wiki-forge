# Sources

Registry of all documentary sources ingested into the project (folder `raw/`,
converted to Markdown and summarized in the `wiki/`). Each entry reports:
author, title, year, location of the full text (`raw/..._COMPILED.md`), and,
where useful, the corresponding synthesis article in the wiki.

The sources follow the thesis line: **Graeber → Cristianini → Gatti → Karpathy →
Amodei** (from the social meaning of work to the technical transformation of
organization and the LLM-OS paradigm).

## 2. Ordered by topic

### A. Critique of work and anthropology (Graeber → social base)
- Graeber, *Bullshit Jobs* (#1) — meaningless jobs and "spiritual violence".
- Graeber, *Debt* (#1) — debt, morality, and power; the barter myth.
- Graeber & Wengrow, *The Dawn of Everything* (#1) — prehistory, equality, hierarchy.
- Gatti, *ai-lavoro-tre-letture* (#226, section) — cautious critique of "AI = job destruction".
- Amodei, *Machines of Loving Grace* (work and meaning) — the future problem is not "having a job" but "finding meaning".

### B. Nature of AI and epistemology of ML
- Cristianini, *La scorciatoia* — the statistical turn (Vapnik's principle).
- Cristianini, *Machina sapiens* — the encounter with language models.
- Cristianini, *Sovrumano* — ANI/AGI/ASI and measurement of intelligence.
- Karpathy, *Animals vs Ghosts* — LLMs as statistical "ghosts", not animals.
- Karpathy, *The Space of Minds* — the LLM as a point in the space of minds.
- Turing, *Computing Machinery and Intelligence* (1950) — the imitation game.

### C. Macroeconomic impact, risk, and governance of AI
- Amodei, *Machines of Loving Grace* — positive vision (biology, development, peace, work).
- Amodei, *The Adolescence of Technology* — risks: autonomy, misuse, concentration, labor disruption.
- Karpathy, *Power to the people* — reversed technology diffusion: individual benefit.
- Gatti, *ai-lavoro-tre-letture* (#226, section) — media narrative and employment.

### D. LLM-OS architecture, agents, and engineering
- Karpathy, *Vibe coding MenuGen* — democratization of software (the floor).
- Karpathy, *Verifiability* — Software 1.0/2.0; what is verifiable gets automated.
- Karpathy, *2025 LLM Year in Review* — RLVR, jagged intelligence, local agents, LLM GUI.
- Karpathy, *Sequoia Ascent 2026* — Software 3.0, agentic engineering, agent-native infra.
- Karpathy, *Auto-grading HN* — LLM as judge/verifier (eval).

### E. HR and organizational transformation (Gatti, applied core)
- Gatti, *#105* — six HR areas and their limits (legacy, usability, human-machine balance).
- Gatti, *#124* — multi-agent collective intelligence ≃ human organizations.
- Gatti, *#141* — literacy and reskilling.
- Gatti, *#196* — output-first vs process mapping (autonomy to agents).
- Gatti, *#200* — maturity synthesis: data, context engineering, agents, careers.
- Gatti, *#202* — systemic manager (with Aliotta).
- Gatti, *#209* — the right model, not #1; continuous monitoring.
- Gatti, *#215* — adoption as iterative integration (MVP > POC; with Dorsey).
- Gatti, *#223* — critical ignorance as a post-LLM competence.
- Gatti, *#225* — agent security (with Lorica).
- Gatti, *#226* — HR maturity test; HR of agent behaviors too.

### F. Skills and the human role in the AI era
- Gatti, *#141* — upskilling/reskilling and aware usage.
- Gatti, *#202* — big picture > hard skills (with Aliotta).
- Gatti, *#223* — critical ignorance: knowing what to ignore/delegate.
- Karpathy, *Sequoia Ascent 2026* — "outsource thinking, not understanding".

### G. AI security
- Gatti, *#225* — from AI-that-assists to AI-that-acts; identity, governance, shadow AI (with Lorica).
- Gatti, *#105* — prompt injection and GenAI security (with OWASP, Gradient Flow).

### H. Off-topic / archive
- Karpathy, *Chemical Hygiene* — personal wellness guide; not used in the synthesis.
